After registration and sign-in, MewMent provides permanently free access to its software features: schedules and subtasks, calendar, multiple work sessions and backfilling, floating timers, statistics, projects and attachments, Gantt charts and automatic export, local AI session management, the planning assistant, external MCP and cat companions.

## 4. AI providers and separate fees

You may use a local Agent or a model API you configure. Provider accounts, model availability, quotas, usage fees and provider terms are separate arrangements. MewMent does not include third-party model credits. Verification itself may consume usage.

Summaries send relevant session excerpts to the selected provider. Chat sends messages, context and information needed for authorized operations. MewMent’s account cloud is not a relay for these model requests. A local Agent may still use its own cloud model.

AI output may be incorrect. Review summaries, time evidence and task changes. Important or destructive actions follow the app’s authorization and confirmation flow.

## 5. Work content and keys

Tasks, projects, time records, summaries and chat histories are designed to remain on your device. MewMent’s account service does not receive that work content or receive or store model API keys.

To reconnect to your service, the app stores its API key in your local operating-system credential store, using Windows Credential Manager on Windows. The UI shows a mask. Model requests transmit the key to the service address you configure for authentication. See Privacy for details.

Choose a trustworthy endpoint and maintain your device and backups. Exports, attachment access and sending support material are your choices. Never include passwords or full keys in feedback.

Uninstalling may leave accounts, system credentials, data folders or your separate backups. Remove provider configuration when no longer needed, and revoke keys with the original provider. Use Contact for account requests.
